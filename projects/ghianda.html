GHIANDA

Fit_nest Wellness Activators for Home and Office

Project Type

Academic project
Concept Design Lab A (Group project)
University of Ferrara

Problem & Target

Back pain, poor posture, stiffness.
Target: Smart workers, sedentary individuals, athletes.

Objectives

Joint well-being, muscle flexibility, correct posture. Design an active seat that combines everyday use with fitness.

Techniques & Tools

Rhinoceros, KeyShot, Photoshop, CNC milling, woodworking, manual prototyping.

An unstable seat for bodyweight exercises and spinal extension.

Ghianda is an unstable seat that can also be used as a tool for bodyweight exercises. It has two detachable parts: the upper part serves as a back stretcher to support spinal extension, while the lower part is designed for more advanced stretching.

Inspired by fitness balls and back stretchers, Ghianda was created for both exercise and everyday use. It is simple yet effective: its balance depends on how it is positioned, and it can be used on either curved side to improve balance and activate core muscles. Made from glued laminated wood sections, it is both lightweight and sturdy.

Sustainability & Methodology: The project required the use of wood as the main material. We focused on the connection between design and physical well-being, using birch plywood, CNC machining, and assembly techniques to ensure durability, lightness, and minimal waste.

Components & Materials

  • S: Birch wood stretcher seat (65x45x11 cm)
  • V: Fixing screw (Ø 4mm x 2cm)
  • P: Steel fixing plate (2.5x4cm x 2mm)
  • R: Birch wood reformer base (45x50x34 cm)
  • B: Steel locking pin (Ø 8mm x 5cm)

Prototyping

The prototype was made using a CNC milling machine on a 2.5 cm thick poplar plywood board. The cut sections were then glued and fixed together with a nail gun. The connection between the two parts was made with a simple sliding lock system. The prototyping phase included testing different design solutions to check stability and feasibility.
