MIMMO

From Fiber to Function: A 2.0 beach umbrella

Project Type

Academic project
Product Design Lab B (Group project)
University of Ferrara

Key Feature

Wind-rotating mechanism, reducing the need for lifeguards to close it during gusty conditions.

Transport

Easy to dismantle for convenient transport. Screw-in base.

An innovative, full-scale beach umbrella designed for multifunctionality and sustainability.

Mimmo is an innovative, full-scale beach umbrella designed to meet specific user needs through a multifunctional and sustainable approach. It is easy to dismantle for convenient transport and features a screw-in base, making it suitable for both public beaches and private beach clubs, where it offers a curious and modern alternative to traditional umbrellas.

A key element of Mimmo is its ability to rotate with strong winds, reducing the need for lifeguards to close it during gusty conditions. It embraces a sustainable approach, taking natural fibers to a new level of function and aesthetic appeal.
