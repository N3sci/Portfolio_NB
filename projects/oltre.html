OLTRE LA SOGLIA

Explorations of Unclassifiable Objects: Zanotta

Typology Exhibition Design
Location Ex-Theatre Verdi, Ferrara
Partners Zanotta, Viabizzuno
Year 2023

CONTEXT AND SCENARIO

This project was developed as part of the Exhibition Design course during the third year of the Product Design program. The goal was to design an immersive exhibition space inspired by Beppe Finessi's reflections on unclassifiable design, as presented in Zanotta – Design: 101 Stories.

The exhibition took place in the main hall (platea) of the historic Ex-Teatro Verdi, located in the San Lorenzo district of Ferrara. The installation invites visitors to discover the deeper meanings of iconic Zanotta objects by challenging conventional aesthetics and perception.

EXHIBITION LOGIC & LIGHTING

The works can hypothetically be visited without a specific order. The hall contains 7 autonomous cylinders, differentiated by size, softly illuminated to highlight their contents.

Cilindri Autonomi

I prodotti esposti nella sala sono disposti all'interno dei cilindri e posizionati su strutture curve di varie altezze da 20 a 60 cm per garantire la massima visibilità.

Illuminazione Viabizzuno

In collaborazione con Viabizzuno, è stato progettato un sistema personalizzato studiando il posizionamento, gli angoli e l'intensità. Il concetto di illuminazione sottolinea il contrasto tra i volumi cilindrici espositivi e lo spazio scuro circostante.
