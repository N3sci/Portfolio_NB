IKEA Toy Redesign

Pickup

Same Base, New Fun: Redesigning children's toys inspired by classic American pickup trucks.

Redesigning an icon for interchangeable play.

Final project for the digital automatic drawing laboratory.
Redesign of an Ikea toy.

During the digital automatic drawing workshop, I learned to use 3D and 2D modeling programs such as Rhinoceros, Fusion360, SketchUp, and AutoCAD.

Pick-up was born from a redesign of the IKEA toy called Lillabo. The basic wooden structure and wheels are the same. But the top of the toy has changed. I designed two pieces that can fit inside the wooden base, and I was inspired by the model of the traditional American pick-up.
